# Market Analysis

## Target Audience
- **Engineering Students:** Need an accessible, all-in-one platform for university projects, learning, and coursework.
- **Hobbyists & Makers:** Looking for affordable kits, easy-to-use simulators, and integrated purchasing for weekend projects.
- **Educators & Institutions:** Require standardized, easy-to-administer environments for teaching IoT, robotics, and fundamental electronics natively in the browser.
- **Hardware Startups/Engineers:** Seeking rapid prototyping capabilities, from simulation directly to PCB ordering.

## Market Trends
1. **Browser-Based Engineering Tools:** The industry is moving from heavy desktop software (Altium, Eagle) to web-browser native tools (e.g., EasyEDA, Figma for design).
2. **AI-Assisted Development:** Copilot-like features are becoming standard across all developer environments. Hardware engineering is ripe for AI integration.
3. **Integrated Maker Ecosystems:** Platforms that bundle software and hardware (like Arduino and Adafruit) command strong brand loyalty.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Tinkercad:** Strong in simulation and education but lacks PCB design, direct component purchasing, and advanced AI features.
- **EasyEDA / JLCPCB:** Excellent for PCB design and manufacturing but lacks integrated code simulation and step-by-step beginner learning modules.
- **Arduino IDE / Web Editor:** Good for coding but lacks full circuit simulation and component purchasing.

## ProtoLab's Unfair Advantage
ProtoLab's "Simulate Before You Buy" and integrated AI Assistant (Electron) bridge the gap between learning, simulating, and purchasing. No other platform offers a seamless funnel from an interactive "Smart Cart" simulation directly to doorstep hardware delivery, completely guided by AI.
